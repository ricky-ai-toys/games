How to Play 🦊
1

Till the soil. Pick the Hoe (tool 2) and tap the brown dirt to make it farmable.

2

Plant seeds & water. Choose a seed on your toolbar, tap a tilled tile, then use the Watering Can (tool 3) every day. Rain waters for free!

3

Sleep to grow. Walk to your bed and sleep — crops grow overnight and you wake up full of energy.

4

Harvest & ship. Tap a ready crop to harvest it, then drop it in the shipping bin 📦 — coins arrive in the morning mail!

5

Explore! Visit the shop for seeds and animals, then the forest 🌲, beach 🏖️, mountain ⛰️ (gems!) and village 🏘️ (wishes & letters).

6

Make friends. Talk to Ms. Maple, Finn and friends — gift them crops, snacks or cooked dishes to fill your Star Journal ⭐.

Controls

Move — WASD / arrows (or on-screen pad)  ·  Use / talk — E or click  ·  Tools — keys 1–9  ·  Bag — I  ·  Journal — J  ·  Pause — ☰

💡 Tip: the Hand tool (slot 1) does everything for you — hoe, plant, water and harvest automatically. Perfect for cozy days!
