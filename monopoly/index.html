How to Play

  • Roll & hop! Move around the boardwalk collecting $2 every lap.
  • Buy spots when you land on them — other kids pay you rent!
  • Own a whole color? Rent doubles. Add ⭐ stars to raise it more (max 3).
  • 🎁 Surprise cards are mostly treats… watch for the Time-out one!
  • ⏰ Time-out: sit out a turn, or pay $1 to hop right back in.
  • Win by being the last kid with pocket money — or the richest after round 30!
