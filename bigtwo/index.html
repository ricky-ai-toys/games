How to Play 🦊

Goal: Be first to play all 13 cards!

Rank (high → low): 2 A K Q J 10 9 8 7 6 5 4 3

Suits (high → low): ♠ ♥ ♣ ♦

The player with 3♦ starts. Their first combo must include it.

On your turn, play a higher combo of the same kind, or Skip.

After three Skips, the last player to play starts a free new trick.

Combos

  • Single — one card
  • Pair — two same rank
  • Triple — three same rank
  • Five cards (weak → strong): straight · flush · full house · four+kicker · straight flush

Straights: five in a row like 3-4-5-6-7 or J-Q-K-A-2 (no wrap-around).

Stars ⭐: when you win, you earn stars from cards left in buddies' hands.

💡 Stuck? Tap Hint. Skipping is smart — never feel bad about it!
